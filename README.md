# Privacy Policy for RoleCharacter.ai

**Effective Date:** August 24, 2026  
**Last Updated:** August 24, 2026

Welcome to **RoleCharacter.ai** (referred to as "we", "our", or "the App"), developed by Claudify Studios. We are committed to protecting your personal privacy and providing a secure, entertaining, and immersive artificial intelligence roleplay experience.

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application across Google Android devices.

---

## 1. Information We Collect

### A. Information You Provide to Us
- **Account Information:** When you sign in using Google Sign-In or Anonymous Guest Mode, we collect basic authentication tokens provided securely via Google Firebase Authentication (such as your display name, email address, and unique user ID).
- **Player Persona Profile:** Custom profile information you choose to enter within the App, including your preferred character name, pronouns/gender, title/role, and optional background narrative.
- **User Prompts & Chat Messages:** Text inputs, dialogue, and in-game roleplay actions that you send during your conversational interactions with fictional characters.

### B. Automatically Collected Information & Device Data
- **Device & Usage Identifiers:** Information regarding your mobile device hardware, operating system version, device language/locale, and general app interaction metrics.
- **Advertising Identifiers:** Google Advertising ID (GAID) collected automatically by Google AdMob for the purpose of serving personalized or non-personalized advertisements and rewarding in-game energy.

---

## 2. How We Use Your Information

We utilize the collected information strictly for the following purposes:
1. **Delivering Generative AI Gameplay:** Processing your conversational prompts via Google Gemini AI models to generate interactive, context-aware dialogue and narrative actions in real time.
2. **Managing Authoritative Game State:** Persisting your relationship scores (Affection, Trust, Respect), in-game item inventory, and quest completion progress locally and securely on your device using Android Room SQLite databases.
3. **Monetization & Energy Economy:** Serving Banner and Rewarded Video Ads via Google AdMob to provide free daily energy and rewarded message credits.
4. **App Security & Policy Enforcement:** Enabling in-app reporting tools to detect, flag, and mitigate inappropriate or offensive content in compliance with Google Play's Generative AI policies.

---

## 3. Third-Party Services & Data Processors

We partner with industry-leading third-party service providers that process data in accordance with strict confidentiality and security standards:

- **Google Firebase (Firebase Auth, Cloud Firestore):** Used for secure user identity management and session state handling.  
  *Privacy Policy:* [https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy](https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy)
- **Google Mobile Ads (AdMob):** Used for delivering in-app banner and rewarded video advertisements.  
  *Privacy Policy:* [https://policies.google.com/privacy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)
- **Google Generative AI (Gemini API / Google Cloud):** Used solely as an AI synthesis engine to generate character dialogue and narrative responses. User chat inputs are transmitted securely via encrypted HTTPS REST APIs.  
  *Terms & Privacy:* [https://ai.google.dev/terms](https://ai.google.dev/terms)

---

## 4. Generative AI & Safety Disclaimers

- **Fictional AI Personas:** All characters, dialogues, backstories, and world events within the App are entirely fictional and generated by artificial intelligence. They do not represent real individuals or provide professional, medical, legal, or financial advice.
- **In-App Reporting:** Users can flag or report any AI-generated response by long-pressing the message bubble and selecting a violation category. Reported content is reviewed to maintain a safe community environment.

---

## 5. Children's Privacy (17+ Age Restriction)

RoleCharacter.ai is intended strictly for users aged **17 and older** due to interactive romance, dramatic themes, and mature roleplay elements. We do not knowingly collect or solicit personal data from children under the age of 13. If we become aware that a user under 13 has provided personal information, we will delete it promptly.

---

## 6. Data Retention, Security & Deletion Rights

- **Local Storage:** The vast majority of your game progress (inventories, quest logs, relationship tags, local memories) is stored strictly on your local device hardware.
- **Account & Data Deletion:** You can delete your chat history at any time within the chat screen, or sign out and request permanent deletion of your Firebase authentication record by contacting us directly at our support email.

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any modifications will become effective immediately upon posting the updated policy on this page with a revised "Last Updated" date.
